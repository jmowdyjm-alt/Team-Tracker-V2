✈️ Team Travel & Event Tracker Dashboard

A single-page, multi-fiscal-year web application for planning project travel, tracking venue site days, managing count-back operational milestones, and monitoring individual team member workloads and schedule conflicts.

✨ Features

Multi-Fiscal Year Planning: Organize events by fiscal year cycles (FY26, FY27, FY28, FY29 — July 1 through June 30).

Interactive Gantt Chart: High-visibility calendar timeline with 42px day-scaling, month grouping, and shaded weekend columns.

Personnel vs. Project Views: Toggle Gantt chart grouping between personnel rows and project rows.

Confirmation Status Tracking: Set events as Confirmed, Unconfirmed/Tentative, or Cancelled (cancelled shows are automatically excluded from active site day metrics).

Team Member Profiles: Single-pane profiles displaying assigned shows, site days, weekend impact, and individual leave records.

Count-Back Milestones Checklist: Automatic prep milestones (T-60, T-30, T-14, T-7 days) countdown prior to departure.

Schedule Conflict Detector: Flags overlaps between show dates and recorded PTO, personal leave, or company holidays.
